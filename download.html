Get Ensemble.

Ensemble is free for one user on your own devices. The server is free to run on your own machine, and the apps are rolling out through the stores.

Free for you

Use it free on your devices, as one user.

Working on your own is free: every tool, no account, no connection. Add collaborators when you are ready; guests who only view are always free.

  • iPad today through TestFlight; Mac and Windows through the stores
  • No account needed to work on your device
  • Works offline: your files stay on your device
  • Free self-hosted server for your team
Ensemble Server

Host your own workspace

Free, self-hosted. Your team connects to it from the apps and your files never leave your network. Versions are published on a public GitHub release page, so you can verify every download.
