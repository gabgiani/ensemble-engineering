Features

Everything in Ensemble.

One workspace for the model, the drawing, the discussion and the plan. This is what the app does today.

Use it freeSupported files
Canvas and collaboration

Infinite canvas

Frames, sticky notes, text, arrows, tables and file boxes on one board, with snapping, backgrounds and a minimap.

Pen and highlighter

Draw and annotate by hand. An Apple Pencil works on iPad.

Kanban, Gantt and templates

Notes carry an owner, priority, tags and a due date, and feed the boards.

Comments, in text or voice

Pin a comment to a position on a 3D part or a drawing, or record it with your voice. On iPad, iPhone and Mac, voice can become text.

Live collaboration

Everyone in the workspace sees changes as they happen, and you can follow another person’s 3D view.

Undo and redo

One undo for the whole canvas, with Cmd or Ctrl + Z.

Showcase

Pin items in order and present them with transitions and voice narration, or open a live Showcase with a waiting room.

3D viewer

Free rotation

Orbit all the way over and under the part, pan, zoom, and jump to standard views with the view cube.

Section cuts

Cut a part with a plane and see a filled cap.

Measure

Distance and angle between points, and pins that stay on the model.

Look and feel

Colors per face, materials from matte to metallic, lighting presets, contact shadows, zebra stripes and a mesh view.

Assemblies

Hide or isolate parts, using the structure and names from the file.

Saved views and pictures

Save a camera and come back to it, or export a picture of the view at the size you choose.

Engineering analysis

Properties

Volume, surface area, weight, center of gravity and inertia, with material presets.

Manufacturing checks

Wall thickness heat map, mold draft and undercuts, stock and machining allowance, sheet-metal bends.

Version comparison

See what changed between two versions of a part, with exact material added and removed for STEP files.

Interference

Find parts that overlap in a STEP assembly.

Drawings

Drawing sheets from a 3D part

Third-angle views at a standard scale, general dimensions, balloons for your pins, notes and a title block on A4, A3 or A2, exported as a vector PDF.

DWG and PDF drawings

Open AutoCAD DWG (2013 format) and PDF drawings, zoom into the lines and keep the text.

Your files

STEP

Exact solids and assemblies, with part names.

SolidWorks

Parts (.SLDPRT) and complete assemblies (.SLDASM) opened directly, with no export and no SolidWorks.

Documents

PDF, Word, Excel and CSV files next to the models, always at the current revision.

AI

Sketch, photo or drawing to 3D

A first 3D model of simple parts from a freehand sketch, a photo, or a PDF or DWG drawing.

Chat and hand editing

Change the model by chatting, or edit the parts in millimetres. Hinges, sliders and meshing gears show how a mechanism moves.

Your AI, your choice

You connect the AI service in the app settings.

Devices

iPad and iPhone

The full app, with the Apple Pencil, and a second desktop on an external monitor with iPadOS 27 or later.

Mac and Windows

Extra windows share the same workspace, so you can keep a part on one screen and the canvas on another.

Meta Quest

A native VR viewer, and the full app as a floating panel.

Android

The app on phones and tablets, installed by sideloading an APK.

Your data

Local-first

Everything works on your device, offline and without an account.

Your server or ours

Share a workspace from a free on-premise server or from DAFONET, and move workspaces between servers with their version history.

English and Spanish

The app follows your system language.

Engineer better, together.

Use it free on your own devices, and bring your team in when you need to.
