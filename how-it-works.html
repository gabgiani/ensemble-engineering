Technology

How Ensemble works.

Where your data lives, who can reach it, and how a workspace is shared: on your device, on your own server, or in the cloud. You choose, and you can change your mind.

Architecture

Local-first: every device is a server.

Each copy of Ensemble on an iPad, Mac or Windows PC runs its own small server inside the app. Your workspaces open from that server, so they work offline and never need an account. When you want to collaborate, the app connects to a second server, and you can have both open at the same time.

Shared workspaces

How a shared workspace actually works.

01

It lives on one server

A workspace is created on a server (your device, your company server or DAFONET) and stays there: its notes, boards, and every version of every file.

02

You invite people

The owner invites collaborators. Roles are Owner, Editor and Viewer. Nobody can self-register on a server: access is by invitation.

03

Changes sync live

Every edit is sent to the server and out to everyone connected, over a live connection, so you see each other’s changes and who is in the workspace.

04

Viewers cost nothing

Guests who only look around are free and read-only. A plan is only needed when people edit together in a shared workspace.

Moving workspaces

Start local, share later.

Work alone on your device. When it is time to share, take the workspace to your company server or to DAFONET. You can also copy single objects between workspaces you can edit.

  • Workspaces you own can be moved or copied between servers
  • Files travel with their version history
  • The same app talks to every server: nothing to reinstall
  • Local and shared workspaces appear side by side
Accounts and access

You decide who gets in.

👤

First account is the admin

The first account on a server administers it. Nobody registers by themselves.

🔑

Per-person permissions

The admin decides who may create workspaces of their own. Anyone can be invited into other people’s workspaces.

🔒

Encrypted in transit

DAFONET is served over HTTPS. For your own server, put it behind your TLS proxy or VPN, as with any internal service.

File formats

What Ensemble can open.

Ensemble reads the files itself. It does not call SolidWorks or AutoCAD, and it does not need them installed.

FormatWhat you getGood to know
STEP (.step, .stp)Exact solids and assemblies, with part namesThe full analysis, including interference and exact version comparison
SolidWorks part (.sldprt)The 3D part, ready to orbit, section and commentMeasurements come from the saved mesh, so volume and weight are close but not exact. No colors or materials yet
SolidWorks assembly (.sldasm)The whole assembly with its sub-assemblies, copies and part namesHidden and suppressed components stay hidden. Interference check needs a STEP
AutoCAD drawing (.dwg)A vector drawing card: zoom, pages, comments, and the AI 3D proposalThe AutoCAD 2013 format (AutoCAD 2013 to 2017). Hatches, single-line text and line styles are not drawn yet
PDF, Word, Excel, CSVDocuments on the canvas next to the modelsAlways the current revision

SolidWorks files are converted by the server that holds the workspace. Your own device and the Ensemble Server 0.3.0 you run on your premises already do it; the DAFONET cloud will after its next server update.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you share.

Without a connection to a server, no workspace data, no files and no usage of your models leave your device. Sharing is an explicit action: the workspace goes to the server you choose, and only to the people you invite.
