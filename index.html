Collaborate. Engineer. Organize.

Collaborative

The whole review,
on one canvas.

Frames, notes and arrows sit next to the parts and drawings they talk about. Everyone sees the same thing, live.

  • Infinite canvas with frames, sticky notes, text and connectors
  • Comments pinned to the exact spot — in text or with your voice
  • Showcase: pin what matters and walk a client through it, step by step
  • Guests join to view for free; editors collaborate in real time
3D engineering tools

Real CAD data,
not screenshots.

Open a STEP file on the canvas and analyze it where your team is already talking about it.

  • Mass properties: volume, weight, surface area, center of gravity, inertia
  • Wall-thickness heat map and mold-draft / undercut checks
  • Sheet metal: bends and flat pattern
  • Stock and machining allowance
  • Interference check between parts
  • Exact version comparison: material added, removed, holes and radii that changed
Every document in context

Models, drawings and sheets, side by side.

PDF drawings, Word, Excel and CSV files open as live previews next to the part. Keep a file live or freeze it at a revision — and upload a new version without losing the old one.

Project organization

Decisions become tasks.
Tasks become a plan.

Mark a risk, a decision or an action on a note and it shows up in the Kanban board and the Gantt chart. No retyping, no stale status meeting.

  • Kanban with labels, priorities, owners and due dates
  • Gantt: drag a bar to move it, drag its edges to change the days
  • The canvas is the single source of truth
Schedule

See who owns what,
and by when.

The Gantt chart reads straight from the same notes your team is already working in, with owners, durations and today’s date marked.
