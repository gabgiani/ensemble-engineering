New · Drawing check

Does the drawing match
the 3D part?

Select a PDF drawing and its STEP part and Ensemble checks them against each other: every dimension is read off the drawing, measured on the real geometry and given a verdict. Click one and it lights up on the part.

  • OK, out of tolerance, review or not checked, for every dimension
  • The measured value, the deviation and where it falls inside the tolerance
  • The measured faces highlighted on the 3D part, with a label and the dimension line
  • When in doubt it says Review, never OK

See the drawing check →

New · Design simulation

Test the idea
before you build it.

Load it, shake it, heat it, throw it against a wall or put it in a wind tunnel, right on the part and without leaving the workspace.

  • Stress, safety factor and deformation under a load and its own weight
  • Vibration modes and heat, painted and animated on the part
  • Impact at a speed you choose, with the permanent dent
  • Wind tunnel with animated air, drag, lift and pressure on every face

See the simulation →

Your native files

SolidWorks and AutoCAD,
no export.

Drop a SolidWorks part or assembly, or an AutoCAD drawing, straight onto the canvas. Ensemble reads the file itself, so nobody has to export a STEP or a PDF first, and nobody needs the CAD program just to review.

  • SolidWorks parts (.SLDPRT) and full assemblies (.SLDASM), with their structure
  • AutoCAD drawings (.DWG) with dimensions, notes and title block, crisp at any zoom
  • Next to your STEP, PDF, Word and Excel files
  • The AI can propose a 3D model from a drawing

See native CAD files →

VR · Meta Quest

CAD analysis
in VR.

Walk around your real CAD model with the Gantt and the Kanban in the same space. Pin, measure and cut the part together with your team, in one shared workspace.

  • STEP and SolidWorks parts as real 3D at human scale
  • Pins, labels, measurements and section cuts, shared with the workspace
  • Gantt and Kanban floating next to the part
  • Colleagues see your changes live, in the headset or on a screen

See CAD analysis in VR →

New · Live Showcase

Present the real part,
live, in the air.

The presenter turns the 3D part with no menus and everyone in the workspace sees it in real time: the turn, the color, the cut, the mode and the analysis highlight. A drawing bar lets the presenter annotate while talking.

  • Pen, marker, eraser, arrow, text, pins and labels
  • When you finish: save the presentation as a new dated Showcase, add the annotations to the workspace, or discard them
  • A discreet “Presenting · n” status at the top right

One product, three ideas

Collaborative. Visual. Engineering.

Most teams stitch together a chat, a CAD viewer, a drawing folder and a task board. Ensemble is the one place where all of it meets.

01

Collaborative

Everyone works on the same live canvas. Comment on the exact spot, assign tasks, present to a client or a supplier.

02

Visual

See the real assembly, not a screenshot. Take it apart system by system, compare revisions, and present it step by step.

03

Engineering

Analysis built in: drawing check against the 3D part, simulation (load, vibration, heat, impact, wind), mass, wall thickness, mold draft, stock, sheet metal, interference and exact version comparison.

Engineering tools

Real CAD data,
not screenshots.

Open a STEP or SolidWorks file on the canvas and analyze it where your team is already talking about it.

  • Mass properties: volume, weight, surface area, center of gravity, inertia
  • Wall-thickness heat map and mold-draft / undercut checks
  • Stock and machining allowance, buy-to-fly ratio
  • Sheet-metal bends and interference checks
  • Exact version comparison

Organize the project

Decisions become tasks.
Tasks become a plan.

Mark a risk, a decision or an action on a note and it shows up in the Kanban board and the Gantt chart. No retyping, no stale status meeting.

  • Kanban with labels, priorities, owners and due dates
  • Gantt: drag a bar to move it, drag its edges to change the days
  • The canvas is the single source of truth
