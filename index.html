3D engineering tools

Real CAD data,
not screenshots.

Open a STEP file on the canvas and analyze it where your team is already talking about it.

  • Mass properties: volume, weight, surface area, center of gravity, inertia
  • Wall-thickness heat map and mold-draft / undercut checks
  • Stock and machining allowance, buy-to-fly ratio
  • Sheet-metal bends and interference checks
  • Exact version comparison

Project organization

Decisions become tasks.
Tasks become a plan.

Mark a risk, a decision or an action on a note and it shows up in the Kanban board and the Gantt chart. No retyping, no stale status meeting.

  • Kanban with labels, priorities, owners and due dates
  • Gantt: drag a bar to move it, drag its edges to change the days
  • The canvas is the single source of truth
Schedule

See who owns what,
and by when.

The Gantt chart reads straight from the same notes your team is already working in, with owners, durations and today’s date marked.
