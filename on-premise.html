On-premise

Your data, on your premises.

Run the Ensemble server on a machine inside your network. Your team connects from iPad, Mac and Windows, and the files never leave your walls. The server software is free.

Download the serverHow it works
Setup

Up and running in three steps.

01

Install the server

Download the signed installer for Windows or macOS and run it on any machine in your network. Linux is in preparation.

02

Create the admin

The first account becomes the administrator. From there you invite your people and decide who can create workspaces.

03

Connect the apps

Enter the server address in the app. Your workspaces appear next to the local ones on the same screen.

Why teams choose it

Control without giving up collaboration.

  • Workspaces, files and version history stay on your hardware
  • Works inside a closed network or behind your VPN
  • Same app and same features as the cloud: nothing is cut down
  • Invitation-only access, with an administrator you control
  • Guests who only view are free
  • Start on DAFONET and move the workspace to your own server later, or the other way round
Need more?

Air-gapped or regulated environments.
