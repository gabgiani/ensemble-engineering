The problem

Why this is hard today

Students learn CAD in one tool and teamwork in another. Project reviews end up as slide decks with screenshots.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Learn by doing

Built-in tutorials run on a sample workspace, so nobody breaks their own work: the screen and tools, 3D engineering tools, pins and Gantt, documents and drawings.

02

Work as a team

A student team keeps its model, drawings, notes and plan on one canvas and presents with the Showcase.

03

Review with instructors

Instructors join as free viewers, comment on the part and follow the plan.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Guided tutorials in the app
  • Free personal use on the device
  • Free viewers for instructors and guests
  • Real STEP and PDF files
  • Kanban and Gantt for project work
  • Runs on iPad, Mac and Windows
