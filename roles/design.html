The problem

Why this is hard today

Designers spend review time preparing screenshots and chasing feedback instead of designing.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Drop in the model

Open your STEP file on the canvas next to the drawing and your notes.

02

Get feedback on the part

Colleagues comment on exact spots, in text or voice. You see who said what and where.

03

Show what changed

Upload the next revision and compare it with the last: material added, removed, holes and radii changed.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • STEP models with orbit, sections and material presets
  • Pinned comments and voice comments
  • Version comparison
  • Showcase to present to clients and management
  • Background and lighting presets for clear views
  • Tasks and Gantt for design milestones
