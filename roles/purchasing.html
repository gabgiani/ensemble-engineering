Purchasing

Weights, stock and open actions, without asking engineering for a file.

Get early accessHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

Buyers need weight, material and revision to quote, and need to know what is still open. They end up asking for files and status.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

See the facts

Weight for the chosen material, stock size and buy-to-fly ratio, read straight from the model.

02

Know the revision

Each card shows whether it is Live or frozen, and tells you when a newer version exists.

03

Follow the actions

Kanban and Gantt show what is open, who owns it and when it is due.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Weight and material properties
  • Stock proposal with utilization and buy-to-fly
  • Live or frozen revisions with update notices
  • Kanban and Gantt of open actions
  • Free viewer access for external parties
  • Deliverables box for handed-over files
