Assembly Walkthrough

Take an assembly apart one system at a time, then present it. No exploded-view drawing, no slide deck: the real model, in the real workspace.

How it is made

Four steps, no CAD export.

01

Open the parts tree

The tree lists every part and sub-assembly, with copies grouped. Expand, hide, show or isolate any branch with one click.

02

Isolate a system

“Show only this” keeps one group on screen. “Focus” moves the camera to it. Colours and background are yours to set.

03

Split into a new view

Each combination becomes its own card of the same assembly. The original is untouched, and every card stays linked to the live file.

04

Pin the views to the Showcase

Pin the cards in order and press Play. The camera flies from the whole vehicle to each system, with optional voice narration.

Why it matters

Everyone sees the same system, from the same file.

  • Assembly structure comes straight from the STEP file: names, sub-assemblies and copies
  • Views are live: a new revision updates every card, or freeze one at the revision you quoted
  • Comment on any view, and turn the comment into a task with an owner and a date
  • Works the same for design reviews, supplier briefings and customer demos
