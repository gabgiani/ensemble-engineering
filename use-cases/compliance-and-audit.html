Compliance and Audit

Keep the evidence of what was decided, by whom, on which revision.

Use it freeHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

When an audit or a customer asks why a part is the way it is, the answer is spread across emails and meetings. Rebuilding it takes days.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Pin decisions to revisions

Freeze a file link at the revision a decision was made on. The workspace keeps showing that exact version, even after newer ones arrive.

02

Keep the trail on the canvas

Notes carry their author, owner and date. Risks, decisions and actions live next to the part they refer to.

03

Hand over a package

Collect the model, drawings and exported PDFs in a deliverables box, and keep the whole workspace on a server you control.

What you get

Built into the workspace

Ensemble helps you keep the evidence. It does not certify your process or replace a quality system.

  • Frozen links pinned to a specific version
  • Every version of a file is kept
  • Author and date on notes; owners and due dates on tasks
  • PDF export of documents and views
  • Self-hosted server: data stays inside your network
  • Local-first: works offline, nothing is sent unless you share
