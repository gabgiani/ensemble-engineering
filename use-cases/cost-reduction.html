Use case

Cost Reduction

Spot material and process cost while the design can still change.

Get early accessHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

Most of a part's cost is decided in the model: how much material it starts from, how much is cut away, how thin it is. By quote time it is expensive to change.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Know the weight

Pick the material and get volume, surface area and weight for the exact geometry.

02

See the raw material

Ensemble proposes stock — a block or a round bar — with its weight, utilization and buy-to-fly ratio, and marks the one that uses the least material.

03

Reduce and verify

Change the design, upload the new version, and compare it: material removed, weight saved, thin walls introduced.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Mass properties: volume, weight, surface area, center of gravity, inertia
  • Stock proposal with utilization and buy-to-fly ratio
  • Machining allowance per side and choice of base face
  • Wall-thickness check, so savings do not create thin walls
  • Version comparison to quantify what was removed
  • Tasks and Gantt to schedule the cost-down actions
