Customer Collaboration

Show your customer exactly what you want them to see — nothing else.

Use it freeHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

Sharing a whole project with a customer exposes work in progress, and sending screenshots loses the 3D. You need a controlled view that is still alive.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Pin what matters

Select the notes, models and drawings that tell the story and pin them to the Showcase. Order the steps.

02

Present as a client

Switch to “View as client” to see only the pinned items. Choose how the camera moves between steps: cut, fade, slide or fly to it.

03

Invite them in

Customers join as viewers for free. They can look around the live model; they cannot change anything.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Showcase with ordered steps
  • “View as client”: pinned items only
  • Step transitions, with optional voice narration on each step
  • Viewer role: free, read-only, no account needed to join
  • Live 3D, not a screenshot
  • Customers follow along on iPad, Mac or Windows
