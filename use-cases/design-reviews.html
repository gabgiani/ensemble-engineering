Design Reviews

Review the part, the drawing and the decision in the same place.

Use it freeHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

Design reviews fall apart across tools: the model is in a CAD viewer, the drawing in a PDF, the comments in a chat and the actions in someone's notebook. A week later nobody remembers what was decided.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Put everything on one canvas

Drop the model (STEP or SolidWorks), the drawing (PDF or DWG), the spec sheet and your notes into a workspace. Group them in frames: Overview, Assembly, Decisions.

02

Discuss on the part itself

Orbit the model together, pin comments to the exact spot — in text or with your voice — so feedback lands where the problem is.

03

Turn decisions into tasks

Mark a note as a risk, decision or action. It shows up in the Kanban board and the Gantt chart with an owner and a due date.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Real STEP and SolidWorks models next to PDF and DWG drawings, Word, Excel and CSV files
  • Comments pinned to 3D positions, with voice comments
  • Frames, sticky notes, text and connectors on an infinite canvas
  • Notes carry author, owner, priority, tags and due date
  • Showcase mode to present the review step by step
  • Works on iPad, Mac, Windows and in VR
