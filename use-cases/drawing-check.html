Drawing Check

Check the 2D drawing against the 3D part: every dimension, measured on the real geometry.

Use it freeHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

A drawing and its model are supposed to say the same thing, but nobody has the time to compare them dimension by dimension. Someone reads a number off the PDF, hunts for the feature in the CAD, measures it and writes it in a spreadsheet. With a hundred dimensions on four sheets, the check is skipped or done by sampling, and the mismatch shows up at the supplier, or on the part.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Put the drawing and the part together

Drop the PDF drawing and the STEP model on the canvas, select both and start a Drawing check. Ensemble creates a check card next to them.

02

Every dimension is read and measured

The AI reads each dimension off the drawing: its value, its tolerance, the sheet and the view. Ensemble then measures the real geometry of the 3D part (cylinders, planes, distances) and looks for a measurement that explains it. The AI only transcribes; it never measures and never decides.

03

Walk the list, see it on the part

Click a dimension, on the drawing or in the list. The sheet changes, the faces that were measured light up on the 3D part, the camera turns to them and a label shows the drawn value and the measured one.

What you get

Built into the workspace

This is a first pass that points a reviewer to what to look at. It does not replace the quality department's inspection.

  • One verdict per dimension: OK, Out of tolerance, Review or Not checked, with a counter for each that also works as a filter
  • The measured value next to the nominal, the deviation, and a bar that shows where it falls inside the tolerance
  • When the drawing writes no tolerance, it says so instead of inventing one
  • Every measurement that could explain a dimension is listed, with its axis and how many faces it uses
  • An overview paints every checked dimension on the part in its verdict color; picking one shows its label and its dimension line
  • When in doubt it says Review, never OK: a dimension is OK only when one measurement explains it without ambiguity
  • Free-form surfaces (splines) are not measured: those dimensions stay in Review
  • Works on STEP parts, with the drawing as a PDF; you connect the AI service that reads the drawing in the app settings
  • The result lives in the workspace, so the whole team and the supplier see the same check, and the labels also show in the VR viewer
