Manufacturability Analysis

Find thin walls, undercuts and costly stock before the drawing is released.

Use it freeHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

Manufacturing problems are cheapest to fix while the design is still a model. Too often they are found at the quote, or worse, on the shop floor, because nobody ran the check.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Open the part where the team is

Analysis is built into the 3D card on the canvas. No export, no extra license, no separate viewer.

02

Run the checks

Wall thickness against your minimum, mold draft and undercuts along the best opening direction, stock and machining allowance, bends and flat pattern for sheet metal.

03

Paint the result on the part

Thin walls and trapped faces are colored directly on the 3D model, so everyone sees exactly where the problem is — then assign it as a task.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Wall thickness with an adjustable minimum and a heat map on the part
  • Mold draft: faces that release, need more draft, or are undercut
  • Opening direction: best axis or X / Y / Z, with minimum draft angle
  • Stock selection and machining allowance per side
  • Sheet-metal bends and K-factor
  • Interference check between the parts of an assembly
