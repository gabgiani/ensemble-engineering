Showcase Presentations

Turn a workspace into a guided presentation, with the real model.

Use it freeHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

Presentations get rebuilt as slides, so the real model and the real numbers are left behind, and the deck is out of date by the next revision.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Pin the story

Select notes, models and drawings and pin them in the order you want to tell them.

02

Choose how to move

Set the transition between steps: cut, fade, slide or fly to it. Record a voice narration on any step so it can play on its own.

03

Present live

Press Play and the camera moves through the pinned items on the real workspace. Switch to “View as client” to show pinned items only.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Ordered steps from pinned items
  • Cut, fade, slide and fly-to transitions
  • Voice narration per step, with automatic advance
  • “View as client”: pinned items only
  • Live 3D models and documents inside the presentation
  • Always the current revision, because it is the workspace
  • Live Showcase: the presenter turns the 3D part in the air and everyone sees turn, color, cut, mode and analysis highlight in real time
  • Drawing bar for the presenter: pen, marker, eraser, arrow, text, pins and labels
  • At the end: save as a new dated Showcase, add the annotations to the workspace, or discard them
  • In VR, see the 3D part in the air and walk around it (read-only)
