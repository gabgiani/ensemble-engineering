Supplier Collaboration

Send suppliers the model, the drawing and the questions in one link.

Use it freeHow it works
The problem

Why this is hard today

Quotes and clarifications with suppliers travel as email attachments. Versions get mixed up, and the answer to “which revision is this quote for?” is a guess.

How Ensemble helps

From scattered files to one workspace.

01

Share one workspace

Invite the supplier to a workspace that holds the current model, the drawing and a short list of open questions.

02

Keep them on the right revision

Link files live so they always open the latest version, or freeze a link at a specific revision when a quote must not move.

03

Close the loop

Their questions and your answers sit on the part. Decisions become tasks with an owner and a date you can both see.

What you get

Built into the workspace

  • Live or frozen links to each file
  • “Update available” notice when a frozen link has a newer version
  • Comments, including voice, pinned to the part
  • Deliverables box that collects the files you hand over
  • Viewers free; editors when you need them to work in the workspace
  • Run on your own company server if files must stay on your network
